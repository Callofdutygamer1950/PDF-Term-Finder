PDF Term Finder – Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

PDF Term Finder is a browser extension for Firefox and Chrome that highlights a list of words and phrases in PDF documents. This policy explains what it does with information.

What the extension collects

Nothing. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or browsing data. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Your term lists (presets) and settings are saved in your browser's local extension storage on your own device. They are not synced to other devices or sent anywhere. Removing the extension deletes them.
  • PDFs you open in the extension are downloaded and processed entirely inside your browser, including text recognition (OCR) of image-only pages. No part of a PDF, and no search result, leaves your device.

Network access

The only network requests the extension makes are to download the PDF you choose to open, from the address of that PDF. All program code and OCR language data are included in the extension and are never downloaded from elsewhere.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your term lists and settings on your device.
  • activeTab: to read the address of the PDF in the current tab when you click the extension's button.
  • menus / contextMenus: to add “Open link in PDF Term Finder” and “Find my terms in this PDF” to the right-click menu.
  • Access to all websites: to download a PDF you chose to open, which may be hosted on any site. The extension does not read or change other web pages.

Children

The extension does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Earlier versions are kept in the project's public history on GitHub.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the extension: open an issue at github.com/Callofdutygamer1950/PDF-Term-Finder/issues.
