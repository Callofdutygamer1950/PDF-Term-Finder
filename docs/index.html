PDF Term Finder

A Firefox and Chrome extension that highlights your list of key terms in any PDF, shows every match with the text around it, and takes you straight to each one.

Download Setup guide Source code

What it does

  • Highlights each of your terms in its own color, with a side panel of counts and every match in context.
  • Wildcards (contamin*, wom?n), phrases split across lines, and simple word endings.
  • Saved presets, so you can switch between term lists for different projects.
  • Reads image-only pages, like scans or agendas saved as pictures, with built-in OCR.
  • Works in Firefox (desktop and Android) and Chrome, Edge and Brave (desktop).

Private by design

Everything happens inside your browser. Your PDFs and term lists never leave your device, and there's no account, tracking or analytics. Read the privacy policy.
