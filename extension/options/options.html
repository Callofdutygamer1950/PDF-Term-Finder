PDF Term Finder

Keep a named list of words and phrases for each purpose, and switch between them here or in the finder's side panel.

How matching works

  • Upper and lower case are treated the same.
  • Phrases work, even when the words are split across lines in the PDF.
  • * stands for any letters, so contamin* finds “contaminated” and “contamination”, and *ation finds words ending in “ation”.
  • ? stands for exactly one letter, so wom?n finds “woman” and “women”.
  • Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored.

How to use it

  1. Open a PDF in Firefox as usual.
  2. Click the PDF Term Finder button in the toolbar (on Android: the ⋮ menu → Extensions → PDF Term Finder).
  3. The PDF reopens with your terms highlighted. Use the panel to jump between matches and read their context.

PDFs saved on your device: open the finder and use Open file, or drag the file onto it.

Your terms are stored only in this browser. The add-on never sends your terms or PDFs anywhere.
